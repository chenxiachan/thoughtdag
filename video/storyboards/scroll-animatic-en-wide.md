# ThoughtDAG — English widescreen story film

Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 30.5 seconds.

This version follows one continuous research task rather than presenting a
sequence of product features. The same paper, question, branches, and answer
remain on screen from setup through resolution.

| Time | Story beat | On-screen copy | Visual causality |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:02.5 | Setup | ONE PAPER. ONE QUESTION. | A question is asked from a highlighted passage in one paper. |
| 0:02.5–0:06.5 | Escalation | THEN MORE QUESTIONS. | Evidence, an image region, a counterexample, and a detour accumulate in the same scrolling conversation. |
| 0:06.5–0:10 | Conflict | WHAT WILL THE MODEL REMEMBER NEXT? | The conversation keeps growing while the next request remains hidden inside the transcript. |
| 0:10–0:12.5 | Narrative drop | I COULDN’T SEE THE CONTEXT ANYMORE. | Motion and clap rhythm stop; the crowded transcript falls behind a clean wash. |
| 0:12.5–0:16 | Pivot | SO I TURNED THE CONVERSATION INTO A MAP. | The same cards move out of the chat stack and become one visible graph. |
| 0:16–0:22 | Action | KEEP THE THOUGHT. SHAPE THE CONTEXT. | The detour remains on the canvas while its edge is cut; the three relevant paths merge into the next answer. |
| 0:22–0:25.5 | Resolution | NOW I CAN EDIT WHAT AI REMEMBERS NEXT. | The answer regenerates from only the selected evidence paths. |
| 0:25.5–0:30.5 | Product truth | THE GRAPH IS THE CONTEXT | The claim resolves into the ThoughtDAG logo and GitHub search prompt. |

## Narration script

The current render is music-led and leaves narration unrendered until a
human-quality voice is selected.

> I was trying to understand one paper.  
> But one question became many.  
> The conversation kept growing, and I could no longer see what the model would remember.  
> So I turned the conversation into a map.  
> I could keep an idea, remove it from the next answer, and bring only the evidence that mattered forward.  
> Now I’m not just editing the conversation.  
> I’m editing the context.
