SECTION 3 · LONG CONTEXT

Position bias in long-context reasoning

Experimental notes · page 12

Models do not use every part of a long context equally. Evidence placed near the beginning or the end is often recovered more reliably than information buried in the middle.

The practical problem is therefore not only how much context fits, but which parts are made explicit at the moment of generation.
